About Me

I Turn User Problems Into Elegant Solutions

I'm Ayesha Sheikh — a UI/UX Designer and Graphic Designer with a B.E. in Computer Science from RGPV University. Currently working full-time as a UI/UX Designer at Satya Kabir E-Solutions in Bhopal.

I blend user research, intuitive design, and frontend know-how to create digital products that are both beautiful and functional. From e-commerce marketplaces and HR dashboards to fitness apps and NGO websites — I've designed for real users with real needs.

Certified in UX Design by IBM SkillsBuild, I approach every project with empathy, strategy, and a commitment to accessible, conversion-focused design.
